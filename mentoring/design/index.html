Design your career

Two tools for moving from self-knowledge to a plan. The Odyssey Plan explores multiple futures before committing to one. The PDP translates direction into specific actions.

Bill Burnett and Dave Evans describe the Odyssey Plan as three alternative five-year plans for your life — not optimistic/pessimistic versions of the same plan, but genuinely different directions. Map all three before deciding on one. The point is to see what you're choosing between.

Plan B

If Plan A isn't available, or if you decided not to do it — what would you do instead? A genuinely different direction, not a fallback version of Plan A.

Plan C

If money and reputation weren't factors — if you couldn't fail and no one was watching — what would you do? This one is allowed to feel unrealistic.

Which plan did you describe with the most energy? Which one surprised you? What would need to be true to make Plan C more possible?
