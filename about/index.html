About

Hello. Thanks for stopping by.

I work in sustainability in real estate: making building operations more energy efficient and decarbonizing new construction by using less (and better) building materials.

Across different markets, the same questions come up consistently: building a credible business case, mitigating climate risk, and getting traction with teams where sustainability isn't intuitive. These tools are for those situations.

I'm trained as an architect, and still recovering from that episode. I love form with function, the idea of wabi-sabi, working with nature. Beauty and delight are the goal; colour, craft, nature and materials are the palette.

If something here is useful to you, that's the point. If you see a gap, the suggestion box is real. You can also email me at theworkingground@gmail.com if you have a more complex suggestion.

Connect with me on LinkedIn or follow my slow living (and very slowly building) portfolio on Instagram.
